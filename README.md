# GST Invoice, Quotation & Bill Maker

Create professional **GST Tax Invoices**, **Quotations / Estimates**, and **UPI QR Payment Receipts** in 30 seconds. Works 100% offline in your browser or as an Android app.

## Key Features
- **1-Click Inline Bill Header Editing:** Customize your Business Name, Logo initials, Phone, Address, GSTIN, Invoice #, and Date directly on the invoice.
- **Automatic GST & Discount Calculation:** Supports 0%, 5%, 12%, 18%, and 28% GST (CGST + SGST), VAT, or Sales Tax.
- **Dynamic UPI QR Code:** Generates a visual UPI QR code and 1-tap WhatsApp payment link for the exact Grand Total.
- **Print A4 PDF & Share on WhatsApp:** Save clean A4 PDF invoices or send itemized bills on WhatsApp.
